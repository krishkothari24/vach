# Privacy statement draft

This draft describes the current app implementation. Review it against the final release build, store requirements, and any added services before publishing it.

Vach Daily stores your language choice, source choice, and saved readings on your device. The app does not require an account. Its bundled reading catalog and daily selection work without a network connection. The current app code does not send these choices or saved readings to an app server, and it contains no advertising, analytics, or push notification service.

If you choose **Open authorized source** on a reading, your device opens the linked publisher website. That website handles your visit under its own privacy policy. The app does not open that site automatically.

You can remove locally stored choices and saved readings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. The app does not provide cloud backup or account recovery. The operating system handles widget display and scheduling.

The final publisher name, contact address, effective date, and store data disclosures must be filled in and checked against signed iOS and Android release builds before publication.
