## Short description

A daily Vachanamrut or Swamini Vato reading on your phone's screen.

## Description

Make room for one teaching each day. Add the iPhone Lock Screen or Home Screen widget, or the Android Home Screen widget, to see a short reading and its source reference at a glance. Tap to open the matching reading in the app.

Choose Vachanamrut, Swamini Vato, or a daily mix. Read in Gujarati, English, or both where reviewed text is available. Save readings to revisit later. The curated catalog, preferences, favorites, and daily selection work on your device without an account.

Widget updates are scheduled by the operating system and may appear after the date changes. Text is shown only when an approved version is available; the app labels editorial summaries separately from verbatim excerpts.
